# Emergency numbers in Japan

**119** — fire and ambulance (free, from any phone).
**110** — police (traffic accidents, theft, violence).
**118** — accidents at sea (Japan Coast Guard).

When you call 119, say:
1. 「火事です」(fire) or 「救急です」(ambulance).
2. The address — save your home and work address in Japanese on your phone now.
3. What happened and how many people are hurt.
4. Your name and phone number.

Many areas offer phone interpreting on 119 — if you can't speak Japanese, say your language, e.g. "Vietnamese".
Afterwards, tell your support organisation.
