# Sorting garbage (example: Kochi City)

Garbage rules differ by city and even by neighbourhood. General guide:

- **Burnable** (可燃ごみ): food waste, dirty paper, old clothes.
- **Non-burnable** (不燃ごみ): ceramics, small metal items.
- **Plastic packaging** (プラ容器包装): containers/bags with the プラ mark — rinse first.
- **Cans, bottles, PET bottles**: remove caps and labels from PET bottles.
- **Large items**: book in advance, usually for a fee.

Tips:
- Put garbage out on the morning of collection day (often by 8:30) at your area's spot.
- Get the **garbage calendar** from city hall or your dormitory manager.
- Wrongly sorted bags may get a warning sticker and be left behind.
