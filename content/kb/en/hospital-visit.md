# Seeing a doctor in Japan

1. **Bring**: your health insurance card (or My Number card registered as one), residence card, cash.
2. With insurance you usually pay about **30%** of the cost.
3. Minor illness (cold, stomach ache): go to a local **clinic** (クリニック / 医院). Big hospitals may charge extra without a referral letter.
4. At reception say 「初めてです」(it's my first visit).
5. Medicine: take the prescription to a **pharmacy** (薬局).

Emergency (can't breathe, unconscious, heavy bleeding): call **119** now.
If you were hurt at work, tell the hospital it is a work injury (労災) — don't use your normal insurance card — and tell your company and support organisation.
