# 育成就労: changing employer by your own choice (general info)

From **1 April 2027**, **育成就労** (Employment for Skill Development) replaces Technical Intern Training. If you meet the conditions you can **transfer** to another employer by your own choice, for example:

- Enough time at your current employer: **1 year** (agriculture, fishery, accommodation, forestry…) or **2 years** (food manufacturing, construction, nursing care…).
- Pass the basic skill test and a Japanese test (around N5 / A1).
- The new employer meets the requirements and has room under its transfer cap.
- **No** private placement agency involved.

Transfers go through your **support organisation** or another official channel. ReWork only gives information — it is **not** a job-placement service.
General information, not a legal decision — confirm with your support organisation.
