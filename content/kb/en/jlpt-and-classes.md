# JLPT and free Japanese classes

- The **JLPT** runs twice a year, in **July** and **December** (usually the first Sunday). Apply about 3 months ahead.
- Many towns have cheap or free **volunteer Japanese classes** (日本語教室), often evenings or weekends.
- Ask your prefecture's **international association** (国際交流協会).
- To start with work words, use ReWork's **"Japanese for my job"** mode — 5 phrases a day.
