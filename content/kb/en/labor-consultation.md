# Problems at work — who to ask

For example: unpaid overtime, your passport is taken, harassment or violence, being pushed to go home.

Suggested order:
1. **Your support organisation** (監理支援機関 / 登録支援機関) — they are responsible for helping you.
2. The **Labour Standards Inspection Office** (労働基準監督署) near your workplace — wages, hours, safety. Free.
3. The MHLW **consultation line for foreign workers**, in many languages.
4. The national body that oversees the trainee / 育成就労 programme also runs a multilingual helpline.

Keep records: dates, hours worked, messages, photos of payslips. **Nobody may keep your passport or residence card.**
General information, not legal advice.
