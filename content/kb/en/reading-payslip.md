# How to read your payslip (給与明細)

A payslip usually has three parts:

**支給 (paid)**: 基本給 base pay, 残業手当 overtime, 通勤手当 commuting allowance…

**控除 (deducted)**:
- 健康保険 health insurance
- 厚生年金 pension
- 雇用保険 employment insurance
- 所得税 income tax
- 住民税 resident tax (from year two)
- 寮費 / 家賃 dormitory/rent, 食費 meals (if any)

**差引支給額** = what you actually receive (tedori).

Check the hours worked (勤務時間) against what you really worked. If something looks wrong, ask your company or support organisation. Keep every payslip.
