# Resident tax (住民税) — why nothing is deducted in year one

Resident tax is based on **last year's income** (January–December), for people living in Japan on 1 January.

- In your first year you have no previous-year income in Japan → usually **no resident tax** is deducted.
- From around **June of the following year** it is deducted from your monthly pay.
- So your take-home pay in year two can **drop** even if your wage is the same. That is normal.

ReWork's Tedori calculator sets resident tax to 0 if you have been in Japan for less than a year (estimate).
If you leave Japan you may still owe the remaining tax — ask your support organisation before you go home.
