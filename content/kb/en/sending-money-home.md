# Sending money home safely

- Use a **bank** or a **fund-transfer service registered** with Japan's Financial Services Agency (FSA).
- **Never** use "underground banks" (地下銀行) or a friend-of-a-friend — it is **illegal** and you can lose your money.
- Compare **fee + exchange rate**: a low fee with a bad rate can still be expensive.
- Keep every receipt.
- Never lend your bank account or card to anyone — it can be used for fraud and you will be held responsible.
