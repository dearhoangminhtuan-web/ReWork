# Tosa dialect (土佐弁) basics

People in Kochi speak **Tosa-ben**. Common words:

| Tosa-ben | Standard Japanese | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 〜ぜよ | 〜だよ | emphatic "you know!" |
| 〜き / 〜きに | 〜から | because… |
| まっこと / げに | 本当に | really, truly |
| 何しゆう？ | 何してる？ | what are you doing? |
| たっすい | 物足りない | weak, unsatisfying |
| おきゃく | 宴会 | a party / banquet |

Example: 「今日はまっこと暑いき、水飲みや」= It's really hot today, so drink some water.
If you don't understand, say 「もう一度、ゆっくりお願いします」(once more, slowly please).
