# Typhoons, earthquakes and tsunami in Kochi

Kochi often gets **typhoons** from July to October and is in the **Nankai Trough earthquake/tsunami** risk zone.

Prepare:
- Water and food for at least 3 days, a torch, a power bank.
- Know your nearest **evacuation shelter** (避難所) for home and work — check your city's hazard map (ハザードマップ).
- Save the numbers of your support organisation and supervisor.

Alert levels (警戒レベル):
- **Level 3**: older people and those who need help evacuate.
- **Level 4 (避難指示)**: **everyone** evacuates.

If you feel strong shaking near the coast: **don't wait for an announcement — go to high ground now**.
